## Government payments
Services Australia administers payments for carers. Carer Payment is an income support payment for people who can't work full time because of their caring role, and Carer Allowance is an extra payment for people who provide daily care. Eligibility depends on the care needs of the person you look after and, for Carer Payment, on income and assets tests. Contact Services Australia to check what you may be eligible for.

## Other help with costs
Cancer Council (13 11 20) can connect people with financial counselling, and in some states with practical or financial assistance. Hospital social workers can help with travel and accommodation schemes for people who need to travel for treatment, and with applications for payments.

## Work and leave
If you are employed, ask your employer about carer's leave, flexible working arrangements or unpaid leave. In Australia, the Fair Work Ombudsman has information about carer's leave entitlements.
