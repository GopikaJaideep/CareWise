## What happens
Some chemotherapy and radiotherapy to the head and neck can make the lining of the mouth sore, red or ulcerated (sometimes called mucositis). It can make eating, drinking and talking painful, and it can increase the risk of mouth infections.

## Mouth care that helps
Clean the teeth gently with a soft toothbrush after meals and before bed. Rinse the mouth several times a day with a mild rinse the team recommends, such as salt or bicarbonate of soda in water, and avoid alcohol-based mouthwashes. Soft, moist, lukewarm foods are easier than spicy, acidic, salty, crunchy or very hot foods. Keep lips moist with a lip balm.

## When to tell the team
Tell the team if the mouth is too sore to eat or drink, if there are white patches (which may be an infection), bleeding, or if a sore mouth comes with a fever. Pain relief and treatments for infection are available.
