## What burnout is
Caregiver burnout is physical, emotional and mental exhaustion that builds up when someone cares for another person over a long time without enough rest or support. It is common among people caring for someone with cancer, and it is not a personal failing.

## Signs to watch for
Common signs include constant tiredness, poor sleep, feeling irritable, numb or hopeless, losing interest in things you used to enjoy, getting sick more often, withdrawing from friends, and resenting the person you care for, followed by guilt about that feeling.

## What helps
Small, regular breaks help more than waiting for one big break. Accepting specific offers of help, sharing tasks with family or friends, keeping a few minutes each day for something just for you, and talking with someone who understands, such as a counsellor or a carer peer support group, can all make a difference. Looking after your own sleep, meals and health appointments is part of being able to keep caring.

## Where to get support in Australia
Carer Gateway (1800 422 737) offers free counselling, peer support, coaching and planned or emergency respite care for unpaid carers. Cancer Council (13 11 20) offers information and support for carers. If you are feeling overwhelmed or unsafe, Lifeline (13 11 14) is available 24/7.
