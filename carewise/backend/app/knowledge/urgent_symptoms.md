title: When to call the treatment team urgently
source: Cancer Council Australia
url: https://www.cancer.org.au/cancer-information
region: AU

## Call the team's 24-hour number or go to emergency for
A temperature of 38°C or higher during chemotherapy, shaking chills, difficulty breathing, chest pain, new confusion or unusual drowsiness, uncontrolled bleeding, severe pain that isn't relieved by prescribed medicine, being unable to keep fluids down, a seizure, or sudden weakness or numbness in the face, arm or leg. If someone is in immediate danger in Australia, call 000.

## Keep the numbers handy
Ask the treatment team which number to call after hours and during treatment, and keep it on the fridge and in your phone. Many cancer centres have a dedicated 24-hour line for people having treatment.

## When unsure
If you are unsure whether a symptom is urgent, it is always reasonable to call the team and ask. They would rather hear about something early than too late.
