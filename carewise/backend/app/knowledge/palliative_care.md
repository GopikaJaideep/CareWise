## Palliative care is not only end-of-life care
Palliative care focuses on quality of life for people living with a serious illness. It helps manage pain and other symptoms and supports emotional, practical and spiritual needs, for the person and their family. It can be provided alongside active treatment, at any stage of illness, not only in the last weeks of life.

## Who provides it and where
Palliative care can be provided by GPs, specialist palliative care teams, nurses and allied health workers, at home, in hospital, in a hospice or in residential aged care. The treatment team or GP can make a referral.

## Planning ahead
Talking about what matters most to the person, where they would like to be cared for, and their wishes for future care is called advance care planning. It can be hard to start, but many families find it a relief. The palliative care team can help guide these conversations.
