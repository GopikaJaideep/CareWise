## Why carers sleep badly
Worry, night-time care, alarms, and a mind that won't switch off all disrupt sleep. Ongoing poor sleep makes stress, low mood and exhaustion worse, and it is one of the biggest drivers of caregiver burnout.

## Things that can help
Keeping similar sleep and wake times, a short wind-down routine without screens, and writing down tomorrow's worries or tasks before bed can help the mind settle. Limiting caffeine after lunch and alcohol in the evening improves sleep quality. Slow breathing with a longer out-breath than in-breath can help calm the body when lying awake. If night-time care is needed, it may be possible to share it with someone else on some nights.

## When to get help
If poor sleep continues for several weeks, or you feel low, anxious or hopeless, talk to your GP. Carer Gateway (1800 422 737) offers free counselling for carers.
