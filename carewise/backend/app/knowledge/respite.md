## What respite is
Respite care is short-term care for the person you look after, so you can have a break. It can be a few hours at home, a day program, or a short stay in a residential facility. It can be planned in advance or arranged in an emergency, for example if you become unwell.

## How to arrange it in Australia
Carer Gateway (1800 422 737) can help arrange planned and emergency respite for unpaid carers, and can connect you with local services. The person's treatment team, GP, social worker or palliative care service may also be able to help arrange support at home.

## Feeling guilty about taking a break
Many carers feel guilty about using respite. Taking a break is not abandoning the person you care for; it helps you keep going and care well over time.
