source: Cancer Council Australia

## Why honesty helps
Children usually sense when something is wrong. Honest, age-appropriate information generally helps them feel more secure than silence, which can leave them imagining something worse or believing it is their fault.

## How to talk about it
Use simple words, including the word cancer, and short explanations suited to their age. Tell them what will change in their routine and who will look after them. Let them know it is not their fault, they can't catch it, and they can ask questions at any time. It is okay to say you don't know the answer to a question.

## Support for young people
Their school can help if they know what is happening. Canteen (canteen.org.au) supports young people aged 12 to 25 who have a parent or sibling with cancer, and Cancer Council (13 11 20) has resources for talking with children.
